Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how TunixBot handles data for the Discord bot and dashboard.

2. Data We Process

  • Discord account data from OAuth: user ID, username/global name, avatar, and locale.
  • Discord server list data required to determine where dashboard access is allowed.
  • Session data and authentication cookies to keep users logged in to the dashboard.
  • Music session data such as guild ID, voice/text channel IDs, queue state, and playback state.
  • Technical logs for errors, performance, and abuse prevention.

3. Why We Use This Data

  • Authenticate users through Discord.
  • Authorize dashboard controls only for users in the correct voice context.
  • Provide playback features (queue, controls, progress, and lyrics).
  • Maintain reliability, security, and troubleshooting.

4. Third Party Providers

TunixBot communicates with third party services such as Discord APIs, Lavalink nodes, music metadata/search providers, and lyrics providers. Those services process requests under their own privacy terms.

5. Data Retention

Session and operational data is retained for service continuity and security. Cached technical data (for example, short lived lyrics cache) is automatically expired.

6. Cookies

The dashboard uses essential session cookies to keep users authenticated. These cookies are required for login and secure dashboard operation.

7. Data Sharing

TunixBot does not sell personal data. Data is only processed as needed to run the service and interact with required third party infrastructure.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect session and operational data. However, no internet system can be guaranteed fully secure.

9. User Rights and Requests

You may request account unlinking, data access, or data deletion where applicable by contacting the TunixBot support channel.

10. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as the service evolves. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated Privacy Policy.

11. Contact

For privacy related inquiries, contact the TunixBot team through the official support channel or website.
